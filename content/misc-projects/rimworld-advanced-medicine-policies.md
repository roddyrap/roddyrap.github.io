"Advanced Medicine Policies" is a [Rimworld](https://store.steampowered.com/app/294100/RimWorld/) mod that aims to give player more control on when to use
certain medicine types.

Instead of having a single toggle for medicine like the base game has the
mod gives players the ability to choose which medicine to use depending on certain conditions such
as life-threatening, infection risk, active disease, self-tend and more.

Additionally, you can choose who to apply these rules to colonists, prisoners, animals, neutrals,
enemies and so on.
