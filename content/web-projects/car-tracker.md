Car-Tracker, available at [carpark.roddyra.dev](carpark.roddyra.dev), is an online website that lets
you keep track of where you parkes your cars.

Add a car in the website GUI and mark where you park it.
You can share cars with other people to make sure everyone always has the most updates parking
loaction, and allow users to mark when they occupy the car.
